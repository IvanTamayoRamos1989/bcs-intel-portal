## How to try

1. Open `/foro#recorrido`. Read the ten-minute order. Print: the walk is its own sheet, and each link keeps its address.
2. From `/gemelo/?theme=vivienda&scenario=water_first&x=pob&y=pobreza_pct`, open the map link. The frame stays on the south, and the water layers’ groups are open.
3. In the chat, ask “cinco vistas” or “agua primero”. The context names the five views and the reply can follow up.
