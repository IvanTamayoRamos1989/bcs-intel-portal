# Deploy — Phase 1.23 what the twin can and cannot do

## What shipped

- `/gemelo#limites` sits under the reading tip. It says Phase 1 can compare the five municipalities, the five themes and the scenarios, and that a blank cell stays blank. It cannot yet draw block maps (AGEB), parcel form, cadastre lots, or fill in a missing figure. It links to `/metas` and `/aprendizaje#evidencia`. It does not link an internal notes file. Figures and scenarios are unchanged.
- Home keeps the chat first. Under the three consultation shortcuts, one card titled “Antes de decidir” / “Before you decide” walks through views, the twin, open questions, and evidence.
- The meeting kit adds one line to `/gemelo#limites`, to read before comparing figures. The password is still not on the kit.
- Chat guide `portal:gemelo-limites-p123` answers “qué no puede el gemelo”, “límites del gemelo”, “límites fase 1”, and “AGEB”. It points at the limits panel, the open questions, and the evidence cards, and ends with a follow-up. The earlier guides still answer their own questions.

`public/stills/` is gitignored and must stay on the deploy machine.

No new public pages. No AGEB polygons, morphometrics, cadastre, or a revenue total.

## How to try

1. Open `/gemelo#limites`. Read the two lines and follow the two links.
2. Open `/inicio`. The card “Antes de decidir” sits under Evidencia, Vistas, and Recorrido. The specialist row is still there.
3. Open `/foro#kit`. The checklist names the limits panel before the figures.
4. In the chat, ask “qué no puede el gemelo” and “AGEB”. Then ask “kit reunión”, “cómo usar el gemelo”, and “cinco vistas”.
