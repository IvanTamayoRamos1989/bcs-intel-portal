# Deploy — Phase 2.1 municipal height and scenario levers

## What shipped

- On `/gemelo` each municipality rises by a figure already on the twin (population, housing registry, air travelers, cruise passengers, poverty, and the other compare axes). The height is relative to the highest figure. A blank cell does not rise and is not drawn as zero. The card names the source, the unit and the year.
- Each scenario has two levers. A lever brings forward a reading already written on the twin (water headroom, the corridor, air demand, cruise kept apart). “Qué cambia” and the link record the lever.
- The layer list can be filtered and shows how many layers are on. Theme groups, source and year stay.

`public/stills/` is gitignored and must stay on the deploy machine.
